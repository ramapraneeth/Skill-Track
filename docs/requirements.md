# SkillTrack: Requirements & Outcome Intelligence Scope

## 1. Executive Context
**Platform:** SkillTrack — Longitudinal Skilling Outcome Intelligence & Impact Measurement Platform  
**Hackathon:** Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Solution  
**Tagline:** *"Track Skills. Measure Outcomes. Shape Careers."*  
**Core Positioning:** SkillTrack measures what happens after skilling.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional skilling platforms measure activity metrics:
- Candidates enrolled
- Training hours logged
- Certificates distributed

However, public policy administrators lose visibility the moment training finishes. This creates:
1. **Drop-off Obscurity:** 30–90 day placement attrition remains unmonitored.
2. **Curriculum Disconnect:** Training fails to reflect live district or national hiring demand.
3. **Unverified Placements:** Informal placement claims without payslips or EPFO records inflate scheme numbers.
4. **Lack of Early Diagnostics:** Training institutes lack prescriptive telemetry to intervene before candidate attrition occurs.

---

## 3. The Longitudinal Outcome Intelligence Loop
SkillTrack operates downstream from vocational institutes to complete the closed-loop governance cycle:

$$\text{Track} \longrightarrow \text{Diagnose} \longrightarrow \text{Predict} \longrightarrow \text{Intervene} \longrightarrow \text{Follow-up} \longrightarrow \text{Measure} \longrightarrow \text{Improve}$$

---

## 4. User Personas & Permissions
1. **Learners / Candidates (`learner`):**
   - Track personal placement records and salary progression.
   - Run transparent skill gap diagnosis against live industry vacancies.
   - Access recommended upskilling sprints.
2. **Training Providers (`provider`):**
   - Manage batch placement outcomes.
   - Conduct 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day retention verification surveys.
   - Monitor accreditation compliance and SMART Grade placement benchmarks.
3. **Government & Policy Administrators (`government`):**
   - Real-time national and state-level longitudinal funnels.
   - Multi-parameter filtering by state, district, scheme (PMKVY 4.0, DDU-GKY, PM-Vishwakarma), and sector.
   - Failure-mode diagnostics isolating dropouts vs. attrition vs. unplaced certified candidates.
