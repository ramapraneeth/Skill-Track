# SkillTrack: Project Specification

**Full Title:** SkillTrack: Longitudinal Skilling Outcome Intelligence & Impact Measurement Platform  
**Tagline:** *"Track Skills. Measure Outcomes. Shape Careers."*  
**Core Positioning:** SkillTrack measures what happens after skilling.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional skilling programs measure activity and output metrics: candidates enrolled, training hours completed, and certificates issued. They typically lose visibility the moment training ends. Consequently:
- **Drop-off Obscurity:** High attrition within 30 to 90 days after placement goes unmonitored.
- **Skill Mismatch:** Curriculum does not dynamically align with local and national hiring demand.
- **Unverified Placements:** Informal and unverified employment claims inflate scheme numbers without genuine wage progression.
- **Absence of Actionable Diagnostics:** Administrators lack early-warning telemetry to intervene before a candidate attrites.

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition
SkillTrack provides the outcome-intelligence layer operating downstream from systems like Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH), implementing the closed-loop cycle:

$$\text{Track} \longrightarrow \text{Diagnose} \longrightarrow \text{Predict} \longrightarrow \text{Intervene} \longrightarrow \text{Follow-up} \longrightarrow \text{Measure} \longrightarrow \text{Improve}$$

### Key Capabilities
1. **Longitudinal Outcome Tracking:** Tracks verified wage employment, self-employment, and apprenticeships at 30, 60, 90, 180, and 365-day milestones with documentary proof (offer letters, payslips, EPFO verification).
2. **Transparent Deterministic Skill Gap Diagnosis:** Mathematically matches candidate competency vectors against job vacancy requisitions weighted by market demand.
3. **Explainable Predictive Intelligence:** Forecasts placement likelihood and 90-day retention with ranked SHAP-style attribution factors.
4. **Prescriptive Intervention Engine:** Converts risk signals into concrete upskilling sprints, bridge workshops, and mentorship referrals.
5. **Causal Impact Measurement:** Evaluates before/after cohort wage uplift, employment conversion, and training provider quality league tables.

---

## 3. Target User Personas
- **Learners:** Skilled candidates navigating job matching, outcome timelines, skill gap remediation, and career progression.
- **Training Providers:** Vocational institutes tracking batch outcomes, at-risk candidates, documentary verifications, and retention milestones.
- **Government & Mission Directors:** State and national policy administrators monitoring training-to-employment funnels, district performance, and scheme ROI.
